How it works

Three steps. One gift.

Fund a card, share the link, and they claim it. Arc escrow holds the USDC the whole way, and nothing moves until the right code is used.

01 / FUND

Create & fund

Choose an amount, add a message, and fund it with USDC on Arc. A unique code is made in your browser.

02 / SHARE

Share the link

The USDC waits in escrow on Arc. Send the link by email, WhatsApp, Telegram or X.

03 / CLAIM

They claim it

They paste any wallet address. Tessera pays the gas, and the code can never be used twice.

Features

Everything a gift should be

Small on purpose. One primitive, a claimable USDC voucher, done completely.

Single-use codes

Each link redeems exactly once. The state lives on Arc, so a screenshot or a second try can't claim it again.

Gasless claims

Recipients paste any address: MetaMask, Rabby, even a fresh wallet. The sender covers the small network fee up front.

Refund after 30 days

If nobody claims the gift within 30 days, the sender can take the USDC back from the contract.
